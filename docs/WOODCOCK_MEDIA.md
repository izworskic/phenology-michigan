# Woodcock sky dance media: provenance and verification

All files live in `public/woodcock-media/` and are listed with creator, licence, original URL, SHA-256, size,
duration and annotations in `data/woodcock-media.json`. `npm run test:woodcock-media` and
`npm run test:woodcock-browser` guard them.

## Recordings
| File | Stage | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| peent.mp3 (0:14) | 1 Peent, vocal | steve b, iNaturalist observation 198609239, Orange County NC, 6 Feb 2024 (first 14 s) | CC0 |
| twitter.mp3 (0:08) | 2 Twitter, wing feathers | Evan M. Raskin, iNaturalist observation 1303476, Haywood County NC, 18 Mar 2015 (0:19.6-0:27.2) | CC BY 4.0 |
| chirp.mp3 (0:10) | 3 Chirps, vocal with some wing twitter | same recording (0:35.0-0:44.8) | CC BY 4.0 |
| sky-dance-full.mp3 (0:45) | complete display | same recording, as published | CC BY 4.0 |

Both observations are iNaturalist research grade with no dissenting identifications. Changes: excerpts as listed,
noise below 800 Hz filtered, loudness levelled to about -19 LUFS, mono MP3. Sounds were not altered or synthesised.

How stages were identified: spectrograms of the full recording show discrete buzzy peents near 5 kHz (0:00-0:19),
then a strikingly regular pulse train near 5 kHz that builds and fades (the mechanical wing twitter, 0:20-0:27), then,
after the recordist's edit, sweeping descending notes (vocal chirps) mixed with twitter bursts (0:28-0:45). The recordist's
own note confirms the order and the edit: "I have removed about 40 seconds of blank audio (bird out of range of
microphone) between the ascending flight twitter & the descending flight twitter." The page shows that gap as an edit.
The peent clip comes from a different, cleaner recording, and the page says so.

Not used: Macaulay Library / All About Birds recordings (playable but not licensed for re-hosting; the Cornell page stays
linked as the reference) and xeno-canto (blocked automated review; uploads default to CC BY-NC-SA, incompatible with
this ad-supported page).

## Photographs
- woodcock-ground.webp: Rev. Lee A. Payne, Jr., iNaturalist observation 108886506, CC BY 4.0. Identification photo:
  long bill, eye set high and far back, cryptic plumage.
- woodcock-flight.webp: Adam Jackson, iNaturalist observation 195811353, CC0. A woodcock in flight at dusk in March (Ohio),
  cropped. Not documented as a display flight, so it is captioned as the silhouette to expect.

No licensed photograph explicitly documented as a sky-dance display flight was found; none is claimed.
